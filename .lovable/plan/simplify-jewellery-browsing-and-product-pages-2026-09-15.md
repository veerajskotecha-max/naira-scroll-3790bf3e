# Simplify jewellery browsing and product pages

## What will change

- Remove product totals from the sticky jewellery category tabs, including the private Golden Hour collection tabs.
- Keep category names such as **All, Rings, Bracelets, Earrings, Necklaces** clean and easy to scan while scrolling.
- Shorten the jewellery product page by removing repeated information and combining closely related sections.
- Keep the high-conversion essentials near the top: product images, reviews, price, size, quantity, Add to Cart, Shop Now, delivery estimate, pincode check, returns, and plating assurance.
- Consolidate materials, care, delivery, returns, and contact help into a compact accordion area.
- Remove duplicate recommendation/content blocks so customers reach reviews and related products sooner.

## Conversion approach

- Do not hide information customers need to buy confidently.
- Remove repetition rather than valuable trust details.
- Prioritize the purchase buttons and delivery confidence before secondary product storytelling.
- Preserve mobile sticky purchase controls, shoppable reels, live stock, and review functionality.

## Technical details

- Update the main jewellery and Golden Hour sticky filters so count calculations and visible count labels are removed.
- Simplify the jewellery product details column and retain a single source for each fact.
- Keep SEO product data and structured information unchanged.
- Verify the shortened page on mobile and desktop, including cart actions and scrolling behavior.
