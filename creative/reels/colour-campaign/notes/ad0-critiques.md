Correct brief.md before Ads 1-5 are built. Re-check BUY2 and BUY3 in Shopify on launch morning, because the owner has changed the ladder three times since 25 Oct.

- **minor** 0.0-11.83 (all footage): The metal carries a warm cast. # v2 reviews (three of five finished before an interrupt stopped the run)

## v2 review: 7/10

- **major** Whole ad: 10 of its 12.5 s are on studio black, and the only other colour is the cream insert at 5.625-6.875 s.: Ad 0 has no colour idea. The owner's latest direction is a CRAZY, smooth colour effect built into the shots, and 'Don't keep it black background always'. This cut is black and silver throughout. It is elegant, but it would be the one monochrome film in a campaign sold as colour. Because Ad 0 is the template, it also gives Ads 1-5 no colour language to inherit. **Fix:** Use the effect the brief already names, colours coming off the stones, because for clear CZ it is literally true: CZ throws more fire than diamond. Add it as photoreal light, screen-blended per the engine update, never painted onto the piece. Keep the stone's table white and put the colour in the flare spokes and on the set around the ring, in pastel chroma only (blush, aqua, pale gold, sage), never neon. Three beats: (a) at the 0.30 s glint, the star splits into a soft prismatic bloom; (b) a scatter of spectral fire in the pavé at 25-35% over 1.25-3.1 s; (c) the hero beat at 6.9-9.4 s, where the centre stone's caustics bloom outward across the dark floor into an eight-fold 'rangoli of light' before the dimension rules draw over it. The cheapest and most real plate costs no credits: shine a phone torch through the real ring onto black card and film the caustics. Failing that, use one veo3_1_lite plate on pure black ('refracted rainbow caustics, slow mandala bloom from centre, soft, photographic', 12 credits).
- **major** 5.88-6.88 s 'THE REAL PIECE'; the turn at 4.0-5.6 s (f_00121); the end card at 9.4-12.5 s (f_00285): The kicker tells viewers that the other 11 s are not the real piece. That is the visible 'AI look' the owner rejected, now written into the ad. The hard cut makes it worse: it goes from the AI ring with a closed, continuous band (whole circle visible in f_00121, 1.5 frames before the cut) straight to the real ring with an open, overlapping back. Seen side by side, they look like two different rings. The end card then ends on the closed AI band again, with its back arc visible at about x 600-700, y 850-1000. The turn was not framed tightly enough on the head to lose the shank. **Fix:** Change the kicker to 'IN DAYLIGHT', which frames the cream set as a lighting change rather than a confession, or to 'NO RING SIZE NEEDED' over 'Open back. / ADJUSTS TO FIT US 5–9'. In the turn, sink the lower back of the shank into black with a 300 px feathered spot falloff below y about 1300, so it reads as lighting and not a mask, or punch to about 2.0x with the head at y 800-1100 so the back drops out of frame. For the end card, spend 18 credits: a nano_banana_2_1 start frame from cushion_hf.png between the two light bars, then a 5 s kling4_0 clip. The decision frame then shows the ring the buyer receives.
- **major** 9.375-12.5 s end card (f_00285): The light bars are the brightest thing on the card. They average 251.6/255 across the upper bar, and 8.7% of the frame is above 240, the same level as the ring's own highlights (p99 251). The ring is about 330 px wide between two glaring white lozenges, so the eye goes to the bars first. 'FREE SHIPPING · COD AVAILABLE' (y 1135-1160) sits on the lower bar's rising glow (background luminance about 152), so 'SHIPPING' and 'AVAILABLE' fade into it. Seven stacked elements in 950 px is more than this brand's restraint allows. Ink 'TAP SHOP NOW' on a glowing rounded lozenge also reads as a rounded pill button, against the radius-0 rule. **Fix:** Grade the bars down 20-25%, to peak about 195, and warm them toward ivory #FBF3EC. Or, as the colour beat, tint them toward blush #FFBDA8 at about 30% with a faint spectral fringe on their edges, so the ring's star is the brightest point. Drop the ladder from the end card, since the chip has carried it for 6 s, and set only 'FREE SHIPPING · COD AVAILABLE' at about 30 px on clean black at y about 1075-1105. Then either set the CTA in ivory caps on black, or keep it on the bar only once the bar is graded down so it reads as light, not as a button. Enlarge '₹2,200' from about 22 px cap height to about 30 px, in ivory at 55%.
- **major** Offer chip, y 1222-1248, at 1.8-5.63 s and 7.17-9.38 s (crops of f_00061, f_00085, f_00121, f_00220): The only offer on screen for about 8 s is set at roughly 16 px cap height, about 6 pt on a phone, and it collides with the piece in every shot.
