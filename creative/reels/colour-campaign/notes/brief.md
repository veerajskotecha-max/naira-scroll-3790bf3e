## Engine update (4 Nov, after testing)
Procedural 2D powder drawn in code reads as digital confetti and fog: tested, rejected. Colour should come from
photoreal sources:
1. **In the start frame.** A nano_banana_2_1 still of the real piece on a pastel rangoli, marigold or powder set.
2. **As AI element plates on pure black.** Powder plumes, falling petals or coloured light, generated alone. The engine
   screen-blends them over the studio-black product footage (shot `blend: 'screen'`), so the AI never repaints the
   piece and stone counts cannot drift.

Procedural FX remain available only for subtle accents: a soft coloured glow behind a piece, a few defocused foreground
petals.
