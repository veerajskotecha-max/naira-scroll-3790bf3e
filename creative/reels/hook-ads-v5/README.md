# Hook ads v5: three 10–15 s ads, each built on one fun, native format

These ads use the same two creator videos as `../creator-ads-v4`. Each one is built on a format people already stop
for, and finished in the brand system: ivory cards, sage type, blush accents, Cormorant italic headlines, Jost labels,
radius 0 everywhere except the imitated Instagram UI, and the flower iris into the end card.

Every ad comes in two versions:
- **Score.** The paid version: her voice over our cleared score at 104 BPM.
- **Song.** The organic version, on one of the owner's earlier songs, with the drop landing on the ad's payoff.

The prices and claims are the ones `../creator-ads-v4` checked live on 2 Nov.

## Sound

`audio5.py` is v4's mixer with five new sounds:
- slot ticks that slow as the reels stop;
- a wooden clack as each digit lands;
- a soft pop when a reaction lands;
- a two-click camera shutter for the freeze;
- a padlock click-clack.

Every sound is felt or wood; none is metallic, after the owner asked for fewer "ting tings". The glints are silent.

The levels match v4: her voice at -16 LUFS; the score at -20 LUFS, ducked 8 dB under her voice. Measured through a
phone band, the music sits 11.5–14.4 dB under her voice. Every master is -14 LUFS with a true peak of -1.4 dBTP or
lower.

## Build

Start from the `../creator-ads-v4` setup: its frames, `lv_ear` extended to 1.05 s with
`ear_frames.py <S> 5.40 6.45`, the product crops (which now include the two single-stone crops), and the voices. Then:

```
cp engine/base5.css $S/ads/engine/ && cp tools/*.py $S/ads/tools/
cd $S/ads && python3.13 -I tools/build5.py $S          # hookA/ hookB/ hookC/
for a in hookA hookB hookC; do node tools/render.mjs $a out_$a
  for m in score song; do python3.13 -I tools/audio5.py $S $a $m && bash tools/master.sh $a mix_$m master_$m; done; done
bash tools/encode.sh hookA final5/A.mp4 16 hookA/master_score.wav   # CRF 16 stays well under 30 MB at this length
```

The engine reads a shot speed of 0 as 1. B's freeze therefore uses `speed=1e-6`, and `at()` honours speed, so the
freeze's leaders stay on the stones.
