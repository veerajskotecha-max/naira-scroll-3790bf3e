# Morph reels: the plan

What we are making: 5 creator reels built from Sonal's, Riya's and Pankti's real footage, cut at the editing level of
the reference (ANATINA's 11.6 s product film). Analysis first, then the plan, then the builds.

## 1. What the reference actually does (measured frame by frame)

It is one unbroken chain of 20 states in 11.5 s. There is no arbitrary cut: every shot's last frame holds the seed
of the next, matched by shape, size and screen position. Four things make it 10/10.

1. **Substitution, never morphing.** Every frame is a real photo. Pieces swap in place on a locked footprint,
   matched in size to about 2% and in position to about 1% of the frame. It is fast at first (one picture per
   18 fps frame), then on twos, then it lands and holds. Nothing melts or blends, which is why it reads as crafted
   and not generated.
2. **Speed is shaped.**
   - Every camera move accelerates into the cut (about x1.37 per frame) and peaks on the cut frame, which is also
     the blurriest one.
   - It then settles with a long, decaying tail.
   - Blur only exists on frames that move fast.
   - The whole film runs at 18 fps, so it feels handmade.
3. **Restraint.**
   - About 43% of the runtime is a finished piece held still.
   - Two grounds only, switched once under the heaviest blur.
   - No text, no glow, no particles.
   - Pacing goes loud, quiet, loud.
   - No transition type is used more than twice.
4. **Sound as grammar.**
   - A quiet beatless pad underneath.
   - One dry sound per kind of change, reused identically.
   - A sub-only whoosh peaks on the max-blur frame.
   - No tings.

Its ten devices: macro pull-back; staccato in-place swaps; shape-match handoff; piece-by-piece build; circle-on-circle
spin-whip; strand wipe; trim-path retract; growing coin series; real-eye zoom-through; a motif relay into the end.

## 3. How we transfer the craft honestly to creator footage

| Reference | Ours |
|---|---|
| Cut-out stills on parchment | Real creator frames, stabilised on the piece, cropped tight so the room stops mattering |
| Swap the PIECE, lock the world | Lock the PIECE, swap the world around it: same hoop, different moment |
| Black ground under the spin | The Naira drawer's black velvet: our second ground, switched once under a blur |
| Eye relay finale | Our flower: Riya's mehendi flower, the lid's flower-I, then the vector logo |
| Wordless | One line of type, only on holds of at least 0.8 s, never over a move |
| Pad and ticks | Our pad (the hero film's strings, beatless) plus felt taps and a sub-only whoosh. No ticks |

Guardrails that keep it from looking AI-made or like a template:

- Blur comes only from the camera move itself, computed across the shutter (no blur filter on a still), on 3-5
  frames at peak speed. Every landing is sharp.
- No optical-flow morph, face blend, frame blending, inpainting, or recolouring of stones.
- Punch-in caps:
  - Riya (4K) up to 2.6x;
  - Pankti's drawer clasp 2.8x;
  - Sonal's 720p 1.6-2.0x on the hoop;
  - everything else 1.3-1.6x.
- Per reel: at most one spin-whip, one zoom-through in and one out, two substitution cycles and one blink chain.
  About 40% of the runtime is a held piece.
- Every reel opens on jewellery or the box with motion in frame 1, never on a talking face. The offer shows by
  about 1.5 s and the deal is complete by about 7 s. Length is 9-12 s.

## 4. The five reels

Each reel has its own lead device, as you asked: "something different for each".

### Reel 1: Riya, "Every stone in its own halo"
- **Lead device:** dark portals.
- **Source:** Riya's 4K original.
- **Pieces:** hoops ₹1,049, Cushion Halo ₹999, Prism ₹2,399 (Buy 3 → ₹3,558).

Beats:
1. **0.0 s:** opens on the hoops in the tray (real 4K detail) and pulls back at 18 fps. Her line "Nobody told us
   demi-fine jewellery under 2500 would look like this" plays.
2. **Hold:** *BUY 3 · 20% OFF*.
3. **Into the hoop:** the camera dives into the hoop's dark centre, cuts on the blurred frame, and comes out of the
   drawer's black velvet, pulling back to the NAIRA lid with the ring. Hold: *Cushion Halo Ring ₹999*.
4. **Onto her hand:** the ring stays locked on screen while the drawer becomes her mehendi hand.
5. **Into the bracelet:** a push into the halo, then the cushion halo becomes the bracelet's centre square in the
   same footprint, pulling back along the strand. *"...the one people stop you for."*
6. **Into the hoops:** her real hair flip carries a whip to the hoops at her ear.
7. **All three:** all three pieces worn: *₹4,447 → ₹3,558*.
8. **End:** the lid print becomes the vector logo and the end card. "You're welcome, but keep it between us."

### Reel 2: Pankti, "Closes with a turn"

## 5. Type, end card, sound

- **Type:**
  - one line of Jost tracked caps, or a Cormorant price with the MRP struck through;
  - ink on light grounds (wood, wall), ivory with a soft shadow on dark ones;
  - only on holds of at least 0.8 s, inside y 270-1250.
- **End card:**
  - ivory ground, sage NAIRA with the blush flower as the I;
  - the ladder stacked one rung per line, then *Applied in the bag*, *COD available · Free insured shipping*,
    *nairaflore.com*;
  - the last frame IS the logo.
- **Claims:**
  - "PVD 18K gold coating", never "18K gold";
  - the ring and bracelet are rhodium, never gold;
  - "cubic zirconia" (her "zircon" stays in her voice only);
  - never Riya's "two years plating guarantee";
  - never NAIRA10;
  - unlisted pieces (Sonal's ring, kada, choker) are never named.
- **Sound:**
  - a beatless string pad (the hero film's own instrument and key);
  - a sub-only whoosh on whips and zoom-throughs;
  - soft felt taps on swap cycles;
  - drawer foley on box beats;
  - at most three kinds per reel;
  - Riya's own voice in Reel 1;
  - no songs on paid masters (Pankti's and Riya's tracks are not cleared);
  - mastered to -14 LUFS, true peak at or below -1.3 dBTP.

## 6. Process

1. Build each reel on the new plate renderer (virtual camera, physical motion blur, 18 fps cadence, piece
   tracking).
2. Run an independent critique of each reel through three lenses: your taste, the reference's craft, and
   ad performance and claims.
3. Revise each reel from its critique.
4. Do a final QC pass:
   - frame counts, levels and the safe zone;
   - every locked anchor checked at 100%.
5. Deliver 9:16 at 1080x1920 and the 4:5 feed cut (1080x1350), with an honest rating for each reel.
