# Lookalike links (1 Nov 2026)

Product pages now link the pieces that look alike in their photos, not only pieces that share a word in their names.
Commit `3c59cd7` on branch `lookalike` (in `lookalike.bundle`, base main `ddb72b7`); it ships on top of the owner-approved cards.

How the groups were found: three finders went through all 134 live photos (stones and colour; motif and construction; the same design sold as separate listings), two checkers confirmed every group photo by photo, a critic looked for misses. 108 candidate groups, 93 confirmed, then pieces that match only in another colour than their own photo were taken out: 77 groups (`groups.json`, full verdicts in `photo-check-result.json`).

A twin (the same design) now leads Complete the look and More; a shared look counts as a set; a pair matched in more than one group ranks closer. The bag's one add-on is unchanged (names and proven sales).

Changed rows on the live catalogue: 49 Complete the look, 44 More, of 134 pages.
