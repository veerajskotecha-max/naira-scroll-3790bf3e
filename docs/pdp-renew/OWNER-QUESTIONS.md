# The renewed product page and bag: what needs the owner's word

Nothing here blocks the preview. Each is a fact or a preference only the owner has;
the page says nothing it cannot show until she answers.

## Facts the page depends on

1. **Returns.** The published policy (and the FAQ) allows a return only for a piece that
   arrives with a manufacturing fault, within 7 days of delivery, settled by repair,
   replacement or store credit; its examples are all garments. Today's page and bag said
   "Easy 7-day exchange & returns" and "if it does not suit you, return it within 7 days".
   The renewed page keeps to the footer's own words ("7-day returns · On eligible
   pieces") and links the policy. On jewellery: is a return for any reason, or for faults
   only? Money back, an exchange, or store credit? If any-reason, the policy page should
   say so and the page can say "Exchange or return".
2. **Cash on delivery.** The site's own pincode list refuses it for pincodes starting
   121-122, 180-194, 202-205, 273-277, 781-799, 800-855, and the islands. The page now
   says "On most pincodes" and no longer gives the reason "high return rate in this
   region". Does Shiprocket's checkout refuse cash on delivery on the same list? If not,
   the page turns those buyers away for nothing. And is 121-122 meant to cover all of
   Gurugram and Faridabad?
3. **Reviews.** Two reviews without photographs (S. K., P. D.) share
   almost every phrase. "Verified buyer" stands on each review and "As featured in" over
   five mastheads: keep each only if it can be shown.
4. **Rating.** The page says 4.5/5 and 5,000+ clients (the owner's figures). Today's live
   page shows "4.9 · 50 reviews" on every product, which no data supports.
5. **Adjustable rings.** 14 rings are open-back and adjust to US 5-9, yet show five size
   chips that all ship now. Does the workshop set the size before dispatch? If not, one
   line ("One size, adjusts to fit US 5 to 9") is clearer than five chips.
6. **Baguette Eternity Band.** Its details say US 5, 6, 7 and 8; the selector and chart
   also offer US 9.
7. **"Zircone stone".** Details say "cubic zirconia". One spelling for the stone, the
   owner's choice.
8. **Top strip.** "Sitewide sale live today!" runs beside an offer the code calls
   permanent, and "Made in limited batches" has nothing behind it in the code.

## Preferences

9. **"Add to bag" or "Add to cart".** The renewed buttons say "bag", as the drawer, the
   offer line and "View bag" do. If "cart" is preferred, every "bag" changes instead.
10. **Buy now while the bag holds pieces.** It checks out this one piece at full price and
    leaves the bag behind. It could become "View bag" in that state.
11. **Title.** A clean left (shown), B serif left, C clean centred: all three are in the preview.
12. **Film grain.** The site lays a grain texture and a darkened edge over every page on
    a laptop. Over the product page it turned the ivory ground grey, so the renewed page
    does not draw it. Say if it should come back.
13. **Customer photographs near the buttons.** A strip of the seven customer photographs
    could sit under the pay marks. Held back until question 3 is answered.
14. **The first sentence under the price.** It is each piece's own first sentence, and a
    few are mood rather than information ("Texture does the talking here."). A short
    factual line per piece (what it is, how big) would sell better; a set already says
    what is in it.
15. **Photo order.** Shopify's order decides it. The worn photograph and the size guide
    come late (slides 4 and 7 on Woven Gold Hoops); worn second would answer "how big is
    it on me" sooner.
16. **Indian ring sizes** beside the US ones (about 9, 12, 14, 17, 20 for US 5-9, to confirm).
17. **Cash on delivery on the first screen.** The top strip already says "Free insured
    shipping"; it is the cleanest place to add cash on delivery, if wanted there.

## Worth testing, not deciding (about one order a day: read taps, not orders)

- The bag's suggested piece: best match (now) or the one that adds least to the total.
- Buy now filled and Add to bag outlined, against the present pairing.
