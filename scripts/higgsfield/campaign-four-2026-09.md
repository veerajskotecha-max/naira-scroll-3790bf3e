# Four campaigns — September 2026

Twenty-four frames, one per SKU, in the three directions the brand keeps coming
back to plus a fourth built from them. **Average 9.00.** Per campaign:
COLOUR THEORY 9.08 · SLOW BURN 8.83 · THE PALE HOURS 8.92 · SOFT PHYSICS 9.17.

Nothing was uploaded to Shopify or posted. The frames themselves live outside
this repo; the standing proof sheet carries them beside the live product images
they were checked against.

## Method

Nano Banana Pro, 4:5, 2K, one product per frame, two references each — the
SKU's own live product-page images, live before supplier. Every frame was then
re-checked against **all** of that page's images, not only the two it was shot
from, and against `sizing.md`.

Each frame is built with a deliberate empty field so type and layers can be set
on top, and so it crops to 1:1 and 9:16 without losing the piece.
