# Three campaigns — September 2026

Eighteen frames, one per SKU, shot in the three directions the brand keeps
coming back to. **Average 8.92.** Per campaign:
COLOUR THEORY 9.08 · SLOW BURN 8.83 · THE PALE HOURS 8.83.

Nothing was uploaded to Shopify or posted. The frames themselves live outside
this repo; the standing proof sheet carries them beside the live product images
they were checked against.

## Method

Nano Banana Pro, 4:5, 2K, one product per frame, two references each — the
SKU's own live product-page images, live before supplier. Every frame was then
re-checked against **all** of that page's images, not only the two it was shot
from, and against `sizing.md`.

Each frame is built with a deliberate empty field so type and layers can be set
on top, and so it crops to 1:1 and 9:16 without losing the piece.

## What was wrong first time

Eight of the eighteen were re-shot. Three of those took three or four passes,
and all three were caught the same way: **scale the frame and the live photo so
one shared feature is the same pixel width, then compare.** Side by side at
contact-sheet size they all looked fine.

- **C1 Prism Rivière Bracelet** (v3) — Floated clean on the third attempt; the plinth that survived two passes is gone and the spectrum fan reads as thrown light.
- **C5 Cushion Halo Ring** (v2) — v1 put the rainbow beside the ring as a stripe; v2 makes the ring's own shadow the spectrum.
- **C6 Charm Box Chain** (v2) — v1's unzip was muddy; v2 closes the seam above the charm and opens a white wedge below it.
- **P5 Baguette Éclat Bracelet** (v3) — v1 and v2 both desaturated the gold to silver against the pale set; v3 holds true yellow gold.
- **P6 Vintage Halo Ring** (v3) — v1 was a generic halo ring — it dropped the two things that make this piece itself: the raised open gallery standing clear of the stone, and the four flat triangular prong-plates gripping it. v2 restored both but the lilac set bled into the stone and turned it amethyst. v3 holds the architecture with the stone locked colourless. The stone still reads slightly rounder than the live oval.
- **R1 Pearl Ribbon Ring** (v2) — v1 stood the ring on a neat wax disc; v2 pours an irregular molten pool with the shank entering it.
- **R4 Star Point Band** (v4) — Four passes, all on one detail. The live band carries a four-lobed clover, and v1 and v2 both drew the focal motif as a five-pointed star — the word 'star' in the brief was pulling the model to the glyph. Dropping the word and calling it a quatrefoil fixed it on v3, but v3 flooded the frame with powder and lost the copy space. v4 holds both: three identical clovers, powder in the bottom third only, two-thirds flat red above it.
- **R5 Heartbead Bracelet** (v4) — Four passes. v1 spilled from a fallen tumbler and the refraction idea was too soft; v2 fixed the idea but inherited the macro reference's fat beads, leaving the 15mm heart reading smaller than the toggle ring; v3 overcorrected the charm to nearly twice the ring. v4 locks it to the relationship the live packshot actually shows — heart and toggle ring the same width, both about two beads across.

### Three transferable findings

1. **A word in the brief can override a reference.** R4's band carries a
   four-lobed clover. Two passes drew the focal motif as a five-pointed star
   because the brief called the motif a "star" — the word pulled the model to
   the glyph and beat the photograph sitting right next to it. Renaming it a
   quatrefoil fixed it immediately. Name shapes geometrically, not by their
   common name.

2. **A pale or coloured set bleeds into the product.** P5's gold desaturated to
   silver against bone plaster; P6's clear stone turned amethyst against lilac
   paper. Both needed the product's own colour stated as a positive fact plus
   an explicit denial of the set's colour — *"the lilac belongs to the paper
   and the flowers alone; not one bit of it enters the stone."*

3. **Lead with the flattest reference.** A macro close-up exaggerates whatever
   is nearest the lens, and the model copies those proportions. R5 inherited a
   macro's fat foreground beads and rendered the 15mm heart charm smaller than
   the toggle ring. Put the straight-on packshot in as IMAGE 1 and the macro
   second.
